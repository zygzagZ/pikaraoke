# UX Audit — 2026-07-06

Dogfooding session of the phone-facing UI, driven end-to-end in a headless
Chromium at iPhone viewport (375x812), with a second tab acting as the splash
screen. Data was backed up to `~/.pikaraoke-backup-20260706` before testing.
Every observation below was reproduced live and then confirmed in code
(file:line references point at the commit this audit was written against).

Related stories: US-53..US-56 were added to `USER_STORIES.md` as a result of
this audit; compliance gaps live in `USER_STORIES_TODO.md`.

## Method

1. Fresh visitor flow: name modal, home (queue) screen.
2. Search flow: library + YouTube search for "Rick Astley Never Gonna Give
   You Up" and "Toto Africa karaoke".
3. YouTube result interaction: thumbnail tap (preview sheet), the per-card
   YouTube / download buttons, checkbox "Dodaj do kolejki po pobraniu".
4. Download flow: fresh download (Toto - Africa, not in library), watching
   the search page and the queue page during download and post-processing.
5. Playback flow: splash screen attached in second tab, song queued from
   library, mini-player, now-playing sheet (lyrics, seek, volume, subtitle
   offset, subtitle source picker).
6. Subtitle source comparison: "Auto" vs manually selected "LRCLib + sync"
   on Toto - Africa.
7. Server log tailed throughout (`--log-level` default); CLI startup also
   exercised.

## Findings

### F1 (P0) YouTube result card: the biggest tap target does the least expected thing

- The thumbnail (~70% of the card) opens a "Podgląd" bottom sheet
  (`templates/search.html:588-593`) which shows a **black empty player for
  ~6 s** (yt-dlp resolves a stream URL server-side, no spinner) and then
  plays the video **with audio on the phone**.
- The preview sheet is a dead end: only "Zamknij", no "Dodaj do kolejki"
  (`templates/search.html:743-757`). After previewing, the user must close
  the sheet, find the same card again and hit the small download icon.
- The actual "pick this song" action is a ~30px icon button
  (`.pk-result-download`) next to a visually identical YouTube-link icon;
  neither reads as the primary CTA.
- On stream-resolve failure the sheet **silently closes**
  (`templates/search.html:612`, `catch(() => closePreview())`) — reads as
  "I tapped and nothing happened".

### F2 (P0) Download feedback: the checkmark lies, then silence

- After tapping "Pobierz" the button shows a checkmark for 2.5 s and resets
  (`templates/search.html:564-566`). The checkmark means "request accepted",
  not "downloaded"; after the reset the card is indistinguishable from a
  never-clicked one (no "downloading", "queued", "in library" state).
- The backend emits socket `download_progress` events with percent, speed
  and ETA, throttled to integer-percent steps
  (`lib/download_manager.py:470-495`) — **no frontend listener exists**
  (grep over `templates/` + `static/`: zero hits). Progress is computed and
  discarded.
- The only visible progress is the `#pk-downloads` box polled every 500 ms
  (`templates/home.html:739-750`) — rendered **only on the queue page**,
  while the user who just tapped "Pobierz" is on the search page.
- After the download finishes, post-processing (demucs, lyrics lookup,
  alignment — minutes on a Pi) is completely invisible on the phone. This is
  the reported "I added a song and have no idea what is happening".

### F3 (P0) "Auto" subtitles served AI transcript despite an LRCLib hit

Reproduced on Toto - Africa (Karaoke Version), CRrZlEF7-SU:

- Log during download: `LRCLib: hit /api/search track='Africa' artist='Toto'`, `tier gate: ... wrote lrclib (tier=line_lrc -> line_lrc, variant=yes)`, `LRCLib: wrote line-level .ass for Toto - Africa`.
- Playback with source "Auto" (default) displayed garbled transcript lines
  ("do bless the rains down in africa i the rains down…") — confirmed by the
  operator as AI output, not the real lyrics.
- Log during playback: `consensus: no sources for Toto - Africa (Karaoke Version)---CRrZlEF7-SU.mp4` — consensus found nothing even though the
  lrclib `.ass` existed, and Auto fell back to the transcript.
- Manually switching the picker to "LRCLib + sync" showed correct lyrics
  instantly.

Expected (per operator and per US-14 precedence): Auto should prefer a
synced-lyrics source (LRCLib) when available, use AI transcript only as the
last resort, and apply quality heuristics before trusting a transcript.

### F4 (P1) Subtitle source picker is a debug surface, not a guest control

11 options including raw error states: "Genius + sync BŁĄD", "Spotify + sync
BŁĄD", "AI BŁĄD", "YouTube CC BŁĄD", "Twoje napisy N/D", plus an unexplained
"3/7" counter next to the selected label and an unexplained "+ sync" suffix.
No indication which source is *good*. A party guest cannot make an informed
choice here.

### F5 (P1) Subtitle offset stepper unusable on iOS

- `.pk-tool-step` buttons are 30x30 px (`static/css/now-playing.css:587`),
  below the 44 px Apple minimum, with no `touch-action: manipulation` and no
  `user-select: none` — rapid taps trigger double-tap zoom or select the
  "+" / "−" glyph text instead of stepping.
- `STEP = 0.05`, range clamped to ±2 s, no hold-to-repeat
  (`static/js/now-playing-bar.js:578-580, 630-638`) — reaching −2 s takes
  exactly 40 taps.
- Manual entry: `<input type="text" inputmode="decimal">`
  (`templates/base.html:536`) — the iOS decimal keyboard has **no minus
  key**, so negative offsets cannot be typed at all.

### F6 (P2) Minor issues

- Toast "Song added to the queue: Toto – Africa" is English in an otherwise
  Polish UI.
- On the queue page, a blue circular avatar/spinner overlaps the song title
  in the "gra teraz" row (title partially hidden).
- `pikaraoke --log-level info` crashes with a traceback —
  `karaoke.py:410` does `int(log_level)`; the flag accepts only numeric
  levels but this is neither validated nor documented at the CLI surface.
- Preview autoplay on iOS is gesture-gated and handled
  (`$video.play().then(pause)` at `search.html:602`) but the black
  no-spinner window makes the wait indistinguishable from a hang.

## What worked well

- Home/queue/search/library navigation is clear; empty states are friendly.
- The name modal is a nice touch and the persistent chip works.
- Library rows expose per-song subtitle source badges (AUTO / LRCLIB) —
  useful signal, worth building on for the quality heuristics.
- Queue page download box (when you know to look there) has a clean
  progress bar with speed and ETA.
- The now-playing sheet (live lyrics, seek with buffering, dual volume
  sliders) is genuinely good once a song plays; switching subtitle source
  takes effect immediately on both phone and splash.
